ABOUT US

Learn more

PMC Art began as a small group of friends who shared one love: making art. The name comes from the people behind it: Paule, the two Michaels (one of them Michael John), Matt, and Chena. Put their initials together and you get PMC. What started as a circle of creatives sharing sketches and ideas has grown into a home for original artworks and custom commissions. Every piece on our platform is made by Filipino artists who care about their craft, from sketches and digital art to paintings, graphics, and plates. We believe art should feel personal, so you can browse finished pieces or commission something made just for you, and talk directly with the artist behind it. At PMC Art, we want art to be easy to find, easy to buy, and easy to love. Whether you are decorating your space, starting a collection, or looking for someone to bring your idea to life, we are glad to have you here.
