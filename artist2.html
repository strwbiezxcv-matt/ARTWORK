About me

A love for detail and texture.

I create portraits, character illustrations, and animal drawings. I use traditional materials such as charcoal, graphite, colored pencil, markers, and paints to add detail and texture to each piece.

Each original drawing is made by hand. Contact me to ask about an available artwork or a custom commission.

Genres

Portraits, anime, comic characters, scenery, animals

Materials

Charcoal, graphite, colored pencil, markers, paints
